About the Brand

Belarte is more than a fashion brand: it's an evolutionary entrepreneurial company, an ecosystem that unites creativity, business and community. Its symbol, the Butterfly, embodies the continuous metamorphosis of art and entrepreneurial soul.

Founded by Angélica Lucas, a Brazilian visionary with an Italian soul, the brand represents the perfect fusion between tropical sensuality and the rigor of European elegance. Belarte builds bridges between cultures, generating economic, cultural and aesthetic value.
